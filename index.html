About

I work solo, directly with the person who owns the thing I'm building.

No account layer, no handoff to a team you haven't met, and no surprise when something needs changing — you email me and I change it. Most of what I build is static: HTML and CSS written by hand, versioned in git, deployed on push. It loads fast because there isn't much of it.

That approach suits a particular kind of client — one who wants a site that works, reads well, and stays cheap to keep online, rather than a platform that needs feeding. If your project needs a database and a login, I'll tell you so and point you somewhere better.

Based in Colorado. Available for new work.
